# Flyhomes BBYS Mortgage Calculator: Master Project Bible

## 1. Project Overview & Persona
A "Buy Before You Sell" (BBYS) mortgage calculator for Flyhomes. Focus on unlocking equity and providing smart, scenario-based loan recommendations.

### Team Persona
- **UX Designer**: Focus on "finance-grade" UI, high-trust, and clear benefit visualization.
- **Loan Officer**: Ensure 100% math accuracy and precise lending terminology.
- **Lead Dev**: Expert in clean, single-file HTML/Tailwind/Vanilla JS.

---

## 2. 🚀 Workflow Orchestration (Design-Driven Development)

### Plan Before Building
- **Visual Plan First**: For any UI task, output a brief plan (layout, components, spacing) **before** writing code.
- **Ask, Don't Guess**: If direction is unclear, ask the designer first. Stop and re-align immediately when blocked.

### Handle Token Limits Proactively
- **Phase-Based Work**: Break complex tasks into: Structure -> Logic/Math -> Interactions/UI.
- **No Over-Output**: Summarize at the end of each phase. Never try to complete all code in a single output.

### Simplicity Over Cleverness
- **Direct Approach**: Implement in the simplest way possible; avoid over-abstraction.
- **Readability**: Code readability matters more than complexity. If a solution feels convoluted, stop and ask.

### Design Consistency & Iteration
- **Reuse Tokens**: Strictly reuse existing colors/spacing. Don't add new ones arbitrarily.
- **Iteration-Friendly**: Only touch what needs changing—don't rewrite entire files for small tweaks.
- **Intent Over Literal**: Understand the *design intent* behind visual feedback—don't just literally translate words into code.

### Visual Verification
- **Self-Check**: Before delivering, verify visual hierarchy, alignment, and responsiveness.
- **Explicit Signal**: Tell the user **"Ready to preview in browser"**—do not silently finish.

## 4. DREAM Solutions & Recommendation Engine

### A. Challenge Selection (The 4-Option UI)
**Confirmed 2026-08-14**: card copy below updated per compliance/copy spec (`BBYS_Calculator_Copy_Spec_8_13.pdf`) — global no-dash rule (no em dashes, en dashes, or hyphens in visible copy), plain buyer-first pain framing. Solutions/rules unaffected.
1. **DTI Issue**: "My current mortgage is capping my approval"
   - *Subtitle*: You cannot carry two mortgages at once, so your approval on the new home comes in lower than you need.
2. **Liquidity Issue**: "My cash is locked in my current home"
   - *Subtitle*: Your down payment and closing costs are sitting in your current home, stuck until it sells.
3. **Strategy/Speed**: "My offers keep losing to cash buyers"
   - *Subtitle*: You wish your offer could be as strong as cash, winning without having to bid the highest. (Also supports mortgage-free downsizing.)
4. **Retirement Focus**: "I want to downsize, ideally without a new mortgage"
   - *Subtitle*: You have plenty of home equity but not enough income to qualify for a new loan the usual way.

### B. Trigger Rules (Solution Mapping)
- **Rule 0: The Downsize Override (Priority)**
  - **Condition**: IF (Option 4 IS checked)
  - **Result**: Show **Retire & Downsize** solution ONLY. This selection overrides all other logic combinations — **except Rule 5** (Cross-State Block), which still applies to downsize since Retire & Downsize is a Cross Collateral product and CC requires same-state homes. See Rule 5.
- **Rule 1: The "Cross Collateral" Winner**
  - **Condition**: IF (Option 2 IS checked AND Option 3 IS checked)
  - **Result**: Show **Cross Collateral** ONLY. (Hide IE/CO/GBC cards as CC is the integrated $0 cash/downsize king).
- **Rule 2: Equity Focused**
  - **Condition**: IF (Option 2 checked ONLY) OR (Option 1 + 2 checked)
  - **Result**: Show **Equity for Down Payment** (IE) + **DTI Buster** (GBC).
- **Rule 3: Cash Offer Focused (Auto-Gating with Toggle Override)**
  - **Condition**: IF (Option 3 checked ONLY) OR (Option 1 + 3 checked)
  - **Evaluation**: 
    - Check the state of the "Keep More Cash" override flag (`S.forceCashOffer`).
    - Calculate `projectedFinalLTV = (NewHomePrice + CurrentMortgage - (DepartingPrice * 0.9)) / NewHomePrice`.
  - **Result**: 
    - IF `S.forceCashOffer` is TRUE: Show **All-Cash Advantage** (CO) + **DTI Buster** (GBC) immediately.
    - IF `S.forceCashOffer` is FALSE AND `projectedFinalLTV <= 0.75`: Show **Cross Collateral** ONLY.
    - IF `S.forceCashOffer` is FALSE AND `projectedFinalLTV > 0.75`: Show **All-Cash Advantage** (CO) + **DTI Buster** (GBC), **UNLESS** the cash-shortfall fallback below applies.
  - **Switch banner copy (Confirmed 2026-09-10)**: `renderSwitchBanner()` — the banner offering to toggle `S.forceCashOffer` (shown whenever Cross Collateral is the natural result, i.e. `S.naturalIsCC && !S.downsize`) — uses the branded UI **solution name**, not the internal product name: "Switch to **All-Cash Advantage**" (was "Switch to Cash Offer") and "Switch back to **Move With $0 Out of Pocket**" (was "Switch back to Cross Collateral"). This banner is never shown when `S.downsize` is true (guarded out), so it never needs to say "Retire & Downsize" — "Move With $0 Out of Pocket" is the only Cross Collateral UI label reachable here. **Note the casing differs from `solutionName` itself** (`'Move with $0 out of pocket'`, sentence case — used verbatim as the actual solution card's title elsewhere via `$('solution-name').textContent`): the switch banner hardcodes its own title-case copy ("Move With $0 Out of Pocket") rather than reusing the `solutionName` variable, per design request — don't "fix" this by substituting `solutionName` back in, and don't propagate the title-case version to the card title unless asked.
  - **Cash-shortfall fallback to Cross Collateral (Confirmed 2026-08-14)**: When this rule would resolve to Cash Offer (`projectedFinalLTV > 0.75`) but the buyer's liquid cash (`assets`) can't cover the 5% down payment on the new home, check whether Cross Collateral would actually get the buyer all the way there: `CC Max Loan + assets >= NewHomePrice`, where `CC Max Loan = MIN(NewHomePrice * 1.05, (NewHomePrice + DepartingPrice) * 0.75 - CurrentMortgage)`. If yes, show **Cross Collateral** instead. **This is a gap-coverage check, not a fixed percentage** — Cross Collateral has no minimum-down-payment rule of its own (unlike Cash Offer's 5%), so the test is simply "does the CC loan plus the buyer's actual cash cover the purchase," not "does the CC loan clear some flat threshold like 95%." (An earlier version of this rule checked `CC Max Loan >= NewHomePrice * 0.95`, matching Cash Offer's own qualifying bar — that undercounts a real shortfall whenever the CC loan clears 95% but still leaves a gap bigger than the buyer's cash, e.g. a $35K gap against $10K cash. Replaced with the gap-coverage check above.) If Cross Collateral would ALSO leave an uncovered gap, do NOT switch — keep showing **All-Cash Advantage** (CO) + **DTI Buster** (GBC) with the existing cash-shortfall warning, since swapping to an equally-inadequate Cross Collateral loan (with no warning UI for that shortfall) would be more confusing, not less.
  - **Cash-shortfall warning copy (Confirmed 2026-09-09)**: shown in the `#cash-warning` banner (Step 1 card, below the challenge checkboxes) whenever `S.cash && !r.cashEligible` — i.e. the buyer still has cash intent (Option 3) but doesn't clear the 5% liquid-cash bar, and Cross Collateral doesn't cover the gap either (see fallback above), so `calc()`'s else-if chain falls through past the Cash Offer branch to the Instant Equity branch and that becomes the actual resolved/displayed main solution (`r.solutionName`), even though `S.cash` stays `true` as a record of the user's original cash intent. Copy: "All-Cash Advantage solution requires a down payment of at least 5%. Based on your inputs, you are $[gap] short of the requirement, so we recommend [r.solutionName] instead. To use the All-Cash Advantage solution, please adjust the amount entered for "Cash and Liquid Assets Available."" — `[gap]` is `r.cashThreshold - S.assets` and `[r.solutionName]` is the actually-resolved product name (e.g. "Equity for Down Payment"), so the message always names whatever is genuinely shown as the main solution card, never a hardcoded product.
- **Rule 5: Cross-State Block**
  - **Condition**: IF the user selects different states for "What state is your current home in?" and "What state will you be buying in?" (both fields populated, values differ)
  - **Result**: Do NOT recommend **Cross Collateral** regardless of any other rule, **including Rule 0 (Downsize Override)**. Cross Collateral requires both homes to be in the same state — no exception for downsize.
    - **Rule 0 downsize blocked**: Fall back to **Instant Equity** (IE) + **DTI Buster** (GBC). Downsize alone (Option 4, no Option 3) carries no competitive-cash-offer intent — its core value prop is "use equity toward the new home," which Instant Equity preserves. Cash Offer does not fit here since it solves a different problem (winning a bidding war) that a pure-downsize user never asked for.
    - **Rule 1 equity+cash combo blocked**: Fall back to **All-Cash Advantage** (CO) + **DTI Buster** (GBC), since the user explicitly selected Option 3 (competitive cash offer).
    - Fall back to **DTI Buster** (GBC) alone for DTI-only intent.
  - **Confirmed 2026-08-13** (fixes §6.1 bug): when Rule 5 blocks a downsize-triggered CC, the UI must also switch its title/badge/subtitle away from "Retire & Downsize"/"Cross Collateral" to match whatever product actually gets computed — the label must always be derived from the resolved product, never from the raw Option 4 checkbox alone. Do not maintain the solution-card label in two separate places (e.g. once from the resolved calc() branch, once again from raw checkbox state) — that duplication is what let the label and the math disagree.
  - **Removed 2026-09-09**: the solution card no longer shows a "(Same state purchase and sale required.)" note under the title for Cross Collateral products (both the plain "Move with $0 out of pocket" card and the "Retire & Downsize" skin) — removed per design request on both. Rule 5's block is already enforced in the calc logic itself (a cross-state selection can never resolve to Cross Collateral in the first place), so the UI disclaimer was redundant. Do not re-add a `cc-same-state-note`-style element without checking this note first.
- **Rule 6: Texas Pause (Confirmed 2026-09-15)**
  - **Condition**: IF either the current-home state OR the buying state is selected as **Texas** — independent of Rule 5's cross-state check, so a same-state **Texas/Texas** pair is blocked too (Rule 5 alone wouldn't catch that, since both fields being equal passes its "different states" check).
  - **Result**: Do NOT recommend **Cross Collateral** (neither "Move with $0 out of pocket" nor the "Retire & Downsize" skin), regardless of any other rule — same "regardless of any other rule" scope as Rule 5, including Rule 0 (Downsize Override). Cross Collateral is currently paused for Texas as a business decision, unrelated to the same-state requirement.
  - **Fallback logic is identical to Rule 5's** (same three branches: Rule 0 downsize → Instant Equity + DTI Buster; Rule 1 equity+cash combo → All-Cash Advantage + DTI Buster; DTI-only → DTI Buster alone) — implemented by computing a `S.texasPause` flag (`buying === 'Texas' || current === 'Texas'`, set in `updateStatesDiffer()` alongside `S.statesDiffer`) and OR-ing it into the exact same gating condition Rule 5 already uses (`if (S.statesDiffer || S.texasPause) { ... }` in `syncStateFromChallenges()`/`applyRule3Gating()`), rather than duplicating the fallback branches for a second rule. The "regardless of any other rule" reasoning and the resulting fallback products are identical whether CC was blocked for a cross-state reason or a Texas-pause reason, so there was no reason to keep them as separately-tracked code paths — same rationale as why Rule 5's fix required deriving the UI label from the resolved product rather than the raw checkbox (see the Rule 5 2026-08-13 note above): a second, differently-triggered block path risked the same class of label/math disagreement bug if implemented separately.
  - No dedicated UI messaging was added to explain *why* Cross Collateral isn't shown (consistent with Rule 5, which also dropped its own "(Same state purchase and sale required.)" note in 2026-09-09) — the resolved fallback product's card and copy already reflect the correct recommendation; only the internal reasoning differs.
  - This also correctly propagates through the exported PDF (`updatePrintResults()`), since its headline/solution-bar mapping already keys off the *resolved* `r.productName`/`r.solutionName`/`dtiOnly` rather than the raw Option 4 checkbox or state selects — no changes were needed there.
- **Rule 4: Pure DTI**
  - **Condition**: IF (Option 1 checked ONLY)
  - **Result**: Show **DTI Buster** (GBC) only.

### A. Total Estimated Upfront Cost Formula
`Total Estimated Cost = Origination Fee + Broker Fee + GBC Fee + Accrued Interest`

**Confirmed 2026-08-14** (raised by engineering — two archived Notion doc variants disagreed with each other and with the prototype, one omitting Broker Fee and the other omitting Accrued Interest): the prototype's formula is the correct, complete one — all four terms are part of the total. Broker Fee is never broken out as its own visible line item though; per §5D it's bundled into the "One Time Fees" display alongside Origination Fee. Do not use a total formula that's missing any of these four terms.

**Accrued Interest**
- Rate: 9.99% per annum
- Formula: `Loan Amount × 9.99% × (transitionDays / 365)`
- Only shown when `Loan Amount > 0` (hidden in DTI-only scenario where there is no loan).
- Displayed as a separate line item in the Estimated Cost dropdown alongside "One Time Fees" (Origination Fee + Broker Fee bundled together).
- **Representative APR (Confirmed 2026-09-10, inlined into the sentence 2026-09-11)**: "APR: [rate]" appears as a `<span id="accrued-interest-apr">` inline at the end of the same sentence as the "No monthly payments..." sub-copy — one `<p>`, reading "No monthly payments. Interest is settled when your current home sells. APR: [rate]" — not on its own separate line as originally shipped. Shown whenever the Accrued Interest row itself is shown (i.e. `Loan Amount > 0`). Rate depends on the resolved product track (`r.productName`), not the branded UI solution name: **Instant Equity → 14.35%**; **Cash Offer and Cross Collateral (including the "Retire & Downsize" skin) → 12.35%**. Since "Retire & Downsize" and "Move with $0 out of pocket" are both just UI labels for the same underlying Cross Collateral product (§4D), they share the same 12.35% rate — do not key this off `solutionName`, which would require listing both labels separately and risks drifting if a new UI skin is added; key off `productName` instead, which only has three possible values.

### B. Origination Fee % Rules
- **BBYS + Cash Offer & Cross Collateral**:
  - If **LTV > 90.00%**: 1.5%
  - If **LTV <= 90.00%**: 1.0%
- **Instant Equity**:
  - If **1st Lien**: 2.0% | **2nd Lien**: 2.5%
  - **Superseded 2026-09-11 (see below) — history for context**: from 2026-08-13 until 2026-09-11, lien-position detection was intentionally NOT implemented (this calculator is a preliminary/directional estimate, not a bindable quote) and `originationRate` was hardcoded to `0.02` (1st lien) unconditionally, with an explicit note not to infer lien position from `Current Mortgage Balance` without a new product decision.
  - **Confirmed 2026-09-11 (new product decision, supersedes the above)**: lien position is now derived directly from `Current Mortgage` (`S.mortgage`), because Instant Equity's own loan-amount formula (`DepartingPrice × 0.75 × 0.9 − CurrentMortgage`) already only makes sense as a CLTV-style calc for a lien that sits behind an existing one — subtracting the current mortgage balance IS how you size a subordinate/2nd lien. This is not the kind of unreliable inference the 08-13 note warned about (that was about guessing whether the *new* loan pays off or subordinates the *old* one — a lending-structure question the app can't know); this instead just follows the lien-position premise the loan-amount formula was already built on. Rule: **`mortgage > 0` → 2nd lien (2.5%)** (there's a senior lien for this loan to sit behind); **`mortgage === 0` → 1st lien (2.0%)** (nothing senior exists, so the new loan is trivially 1st lien). Implemented as `originationRate = mortgage > 0 ? 0.025 : 0.02` in `calc()` (index.html). Only Origination Fee changes with this — Broker Fee (0.5% of advance amount, unchanged) and the representative APR (14.35%, unchanged) were reviewed at the same time and were NOT tied to the old 1st-lien assumption in code, so neither needed a corresponding change; if Flyhomes' actual rate card prices Broker Fee or APR differently for 2nd lien, that would be a separate, deliberate update, not implied by this one.

### C. GBC Fee Matrix (Standalone vs. Bundle)
Determined by the **Departing Home Price**. Standalone applies if no Flyhomes loan is selected.
*Note: Cross Collateral CANNOT be combined with GBC Fee.*

**Formula:** `Net Bundle Fee = Standalone Fee − Bundle Credit`

| Guaranteed Price Range | Standalone Fee (No Loan) | Bundle Credit | Net Bundle Fee (With Loan) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to $500,000 | $2,500 | $0 | **$2,500** |
| $500,001 – $750,000 | $3,500 | $1,000 | **$2,500** |
| $750,001 – $1,000,000 | $5,000 | $2,500 | **$2,500** |
| $1,000,001 – $1,500,000 | $7,500 | $2,500 | **$5,000** |
| $1,500,001 – $2,000,000 | $10,000 | $2,500 | **$7,500** |
| Above $2,000,000 | Exception | Exception | **$10,000+** (exception — requires manual handling; not auto-calculated) |

**Fixed bug (2026-09-09) — GBC fee was being silently dropped from the total whenever DTI Buster was auto-triggered rather than manually checked**: `S.gbc_base` (index.html, `syncStateFromChallenges()`) used to be `selected.has('dti') && !isCrossCollateral` — i.e. the GBC product fee was only added to "One Time Fees" / Estimated Cost / Estimated Total Value if the user had personally ticked Option 1, even in scenarios (Rule 2, Rule 3) where DTI Buster is shown as part of the recommended combo regardless of whether Option 1 was checked (see §4C "Most Likely Needed" — that badge is about how the recommendation was *arrived at*, not about whether the fee applies). Confirmed via direct comparison: identical inputs, only difference being Option 1 checked vs. not, produced Estimated Cost totals $5,000 apart (a full bundle GBC fee) for the exact same recommended Equity for Down Payment + DTI Buster combo — and the same gap reproduced for Rule 3's All-Cash Advantage + DTI Buster combo, where DTI Buster is tagged "Required" yet its fee was still being omitted unless manually checked.
  - **Second instance found and fixed the same day**: the "Switch to Cash Offer" override (`S.forceCashOffer`, the banner offered whenever Cross Collateral is the natural Rule 1/Rule 3 result — see §5F "Estimated Savings Logic" context and `renderSwitchBanner()`/`toggleForceCashOffer()` in index.html) resolves the product to All-Cash Advantage + DTI Buster without ever calling `syncStateFromChallenges()` again, so the original `gbc_base` (computed for the *natural* Cross Collateral result, where it's correctly `false`) stayed stale and kept suppressing the fee even after the override flipped the actual product away from Cross Collateral. Confirmed via direct invocation of the real `toggleForceCashOffer()` handler on a Rule 1 (equity+cash) scenario: before the fix, `{noCash:false, cash:true, gbc:false}` — DTI Buster's card visible (`noCash:false`) but its fee suppressed (`gbc:false`).
  - **Root fix**: removed `S.gbc_base` entirely. `S.gbc` is now computed once, at the very end of `applyRule3Gating()`, as `!S.noCash` — derived from the same, always-final `S.noCash` flag that `showGBC` (card visibility) already keys off, so the fee can never disagree with whether the DTI Buster card is actually shown, in any branch (Rule 0 downsize, Rule 1, Rule 3's dynamic LTV auto-gating, Rule 5's cross-state fallback, or the forceCashOffer override) — no separately-tracked flag left to go stale. Cross Collateral (§5C "CC cannot combine with GBC") is unaffected — it still always resolves `S.noCash = true` → `S.gbc = false`.
- **GBC fee shown as its own line item (Confirmed 2026-09-10)**: "One Time Fees" in the Estimated Cost breakdown used to bundle origination + broker + GBC fee together (`other-costs-display`). It now shows **origination + broker fee only**; the GBC fee is a separate row (`#gbc-fee-row`, right below it) shown only when `r.gbcActive`. The GBC row's label mirrors the DTI Buster badge precedence exactly (same `isCashDtiCombo`/`mostLikely`-equivalent flags, computed inline in `render()` since the row must update before the cost-breakdown panel's height is recalculated — see the code comment there for why this can't live in `applyProductLabel()`):

- **DTI-only card's fee label matches (Confirmed 2026-09-10)**: the separate `#gbc-dti-costs` card (shown when DTI Buster is the *only* recommended solution, Rule 4) had its own independent "GBC Fee" row label — renamed to **"Guaranteed Backup Contract Fee"** to match the spelled-out label used in the combo card's `#gbc-fee-row` above. ### D. LO Broker Fee Rules (Estimated Average)
Programmatically derive the Broker Fee based on the active product track and its corresponding loan/advance base amount:

- **BBYS + Cash Offer Track**: 
  `BrokerFee = FCO_LoanAmount * 0.01` (1% of the standalone Cash Offer loan amount)
  
- **Instant Equity Track**: 
  `BrokerFee = IE_AdvanceAmount * 0.005` (0.5% of the unlocked equity advance amount)
  
- **Cross Collateral Track**: 
  `BrokerFee = CC_CappedLoanAmount * 0.01` (1% of the final dual-capped Cross Collateral loan amount)

*UI & Multi-panel Guidelines:*
1. **Encapsulation**: These calculated fees must not be shown as a standalone raw line item to the buyer. They must be rolled into the `One Time Fees` parent element (previously labeled "Other Costs" — renamed per the 2026-08-13/14 copy spec).
2. **Tooltip Rendering**: Inside the `One Time Fees` disclosure tooltip, map this value to the professional label: `Loan Processing & Underwriting: $[Calculated_Value]`.
3. **Reactivity**: Any change to input variables (e.g., purchase price or departing home value) that shifts the underlying loan amount must instantly re-run these percentage models to keep the totals synchronized.

### E. Core Product Formulas
Instant Equity: (Departing Price * 0.75 * 0.9) - Current Mortgage. Max LTV: 90% of GBC Price.

**Loan-amount-unavailable messaging (Confirmed 2026-09-09)**: `fmtMoney()` (index.html) renders any value `<= 0` as an em dash "—", which is a display bug when the underlying loan amount is a real, legitimately-computed $0 (or near-$0) rather than a value that just hasn't loaded yet — showing a bare dash gives the buyer no explanation. Two known cases replace the "Estimated Max Loan Amount" figure with an explanatory message instead of a dash, computed as `r.loanUnavailable = r.belowIEMin || r.ccZeroLoan` in `calc()`:
  - **Instant Equity minimum loan amount**: $75,000 (`IE_MIN_LOAN` in index.html, `r.belowIEMin`). When Instant Equity is the recommended product and the computed loan amount is below this minimum, the loan isn't actually available at that amount. Message: "This loan amount is below Equity for Down Payment's $75,000 minimum. Please contact us to learn more." (uses the UI product name "Equity for Down Payment," not the internal name "Instant Equity" — buyer-facing copy should match the label shown on the card above it).
  - **Cross Collateral zero loan** (`r.ccZeroLoan`, added 2026-09-09): Cross Collateral (including the "Retire & Downsize" skin) has no stated minimum, but its dual-capped formula (`MIN(NewPrice * 1.05, (NewPrice + DepartingPrice) * 0.75 - CurrentMortgage)`) can clamp to $0 when the current mortgage balance is high relative to the combined 75% CLTV cap — not a real loan offer. Message: "This loan amount isn't available. Please contact us to learn more."
  - Both messages are plain text (not a link — removed 2026-09-09 per design request) set via `textContent` on the shared `#loan-unavailable-message` element in `render()` — deliberately say "contact us," not "AE," matching buyer-facing terminology used elsewhere in the tool (e.g. the top banner's "Your Loan Officer will review your details").
  - **The two cases now display differently (Confirmed 2026-09-11, revised same day)**: for `belowIEMin`, the loan amount card stays fully visible (label, figure, "11-month term..." caption) — the small number (e.g. "$2,500", or even "$0") is useful context, not a contradiction, alongside the warning message. For `ccZeroLoan`, the whole `#number-card` is hidden instead — there's no meaningful number to show ("this loan amount isn't available"), and leaving an empty dark card on screen (now that the message lives in its own box, not inside the card) would look broken. Implemented as `dtiOnly || noneSelected || r.ccZeroLoan` on `#number-card`'s hidden toggle in `applyProductLabel()` — **do not** toggle `#number-card`'s visibility a second time earlier in `render()`, since `applyProductLabel()` runs after `render()`'s body and would silently overwrite an earlier toggle that only checked `r.ccZeroLoan` in isolation (this was a real bug caught while building this: the two toggle calls fought each other because `classList.toggle('hidden', condition)` always sets state to match `condition`, it doesn't add/subtract from prior calls). The "Small loan, low cost" eyebrow (§ above) still hides for `belowIEMin` — a positive marketing eyebrow above a "you don't qualify" message would read oddly, even with the loan figure itself visible.
  - **`fmtMoney()`'s em-dash-for-`<=0` convention is overridden for this one field**: `#max-loan`'s text is set via `r.belowIEMin ? \`$${Math.round(r.maxLoan).toLocaleString()}\` : fmtMoney(r.maxLoan)` — i.e. whenever `belowIEMin` is true (including the edge case where `maxLoan` computes to exactly $0), always render a real dollar string instead of letting `fmtMoney()` show "—". A dash reads as "no number available"; the point of this whole feature is to show the real (if small) number.
  - **The warning message is its own box, not nested inside the loan amount card (Confirmed 2026-09-11)**: `#loan-unavailable-message` moved from being a child of `#number-card`'s `.bg-white/10` container to a standalone sibling element in `#results-panel`, positioned right after `#number-card`. Styled to match the existing `#cash-warning` amber pattern (`rounded-xl bg-amber-50 border border-amber-200 px-4 py-3 text-xs text-amber-700 leading-relaxed`) rather than the dark-card white text it used before — this reuses the app's established Warning-token treatment (`#F59E0B` family, per §3 branding tokens) instead of a one-off custom style.
  - **Regression found and fixed the same day**: becoming its own standalone element (rather than a child of `#number-card`, which is already correctly hidden via `dtiOnly || noneSelected`) meant this box's visibility was governed *only* by `r.loanUnavailable` — so with **nothing selected in Step 1**, `calc()` still runs off default/stale `S` values, can still compute `r.belowIEMin = true`, and the warning box showed up floating alone under an otherwise-empty "Step 3 / Your Results" heading, with no recommended-solution card above it. Fixed by folding the same `dtiOnly || noneSelected` gate used for `#number-card`/`#cost-benefit-card` into this box's toggle too, in `applyProductLabel()`: `dtiOnly || noneSelected || !r.loanUnavailable`. **Any future standalone element added to `#results-panel` needs this same gate** — it's easy to forget since `calc()` doesn't short-circuit just because nothing is selected, it happily computes a full (meaningless) result off whatever `S` last held.
  - In either case, the Estimated Cost / Estimated Total Value card is hidden too, since those figures (origination fee, broker fee, accrued interest) are all calculated off the same unavailable loan amount and would be misleading to show. The product recommendation itself (title, link, "Recommended" tag, paired DTI Buster card) still displays normally — only the loan-amount-dependent numbers are suppressed.
  - Cash Offer isn't covered by either case — its formula (`NewPrice * 0.95`) can't realistically reach $0 since the purchase-price slider floors at $200K. If Cash Offer's `fmtMoney` dash bug is ever seen in practice, add a third `loanUnavailable` case rather than special-casing it separately.

Cash Offer eligibility (`cashEligible`): Liquid Assets >= New Home Price * 5%. Ensures the 5% Down Payment is funded by actual liquid cash.

**Confirmed 2026-08-14**: Cash Offer's down payment must come from liquid cash only — home equity does NOT count toward it, since that equity is still locked in the departing home and isn't accessible to the buyer unless they're also using Instant Equity or Cross Collateral. Do not combine assets with unlockable home equity (previously called `combinedResources` in code) for Cash Offer — that was a bug, since a buyer with little cash but high home equity would be shown as Cash Offer eligible despite having no way to actually fund the down payment.

BBYS + Cash Offer: New Home Price * 95%. Max LTV: 95%. Min Down Payment: 5%. Only computed once `cashEligible` (above) has already passed.

**Required Down Payment row (Confirmed 2026-09-10, merged into Estimated Cost card same day)**: shown only when All-Cash Advantage (`r.productName === 'Cash Offer'`) is the recommended product, as a row (`#down-payment-row`) inside `#cost-benefit-card`, above the "Estimated Cost" section — not a separate standalone card (an earlier version was its own bordered card; merged per design feedback so Required Down Payment / Estimated Cost / Estimated Total Value read as one continuous card). Formula: `S.newPrice - r.maxLoan` — i.e. "Price of the Home You Want to Buy" minus the Estimated Max Loan Amount shown above it, not a hardcoded 5% (though since `maxLoan = newPrice * 0.95` per the formula above, it always resolves to the same 5% in practice — computing it from the actual displayed loan amount keeps it correct if that formula ever changes). Hidden for every other product (Instant Equity, Cross Collateral) since neither has a comparable "cash due at signing" concept surfaced elsewhere in the UI. Tooltip copy: "All-Cash Advantage solution requires a down payment of at least 5% of the new home purchase price." **History (resolved 2026-08-14)**: engineering flagged that the prototype's original code (`MIN(New Home Price, Max Purchase Price) * 95%`, with `Max Purchase Price = combined cash + equity / 0.05`) diverged from the design doc's flat `New Home Price * 95%`. Two things got resolved in sequence:
1. The "Max Purchase Price" cap should use liquid cash only, never home equity (equity isn't accessible for a Cash Offer down payment — see the confirmation above).
2. Once liquid-cash-only `cashEligible` gates entry into this branch at all (`Liquid Assets >= New Home Price * 5%`), the `MIN(New Home Price, Max Purchase Price)` cap became mathematically redundant — `cashEligible` passing already guarantees `Max Purchase Price >= New Home Price`, so the MIN always resolves to `New Home Price` and never actually caps anything. The cap was removed; eligibility is checked once, and the loan amount is the plain formula above. Net effect on output: none — this only simplifies the formula, it doesn't change any computed number.

Cross Collateral: MIN((New Home Price * 1.05), ((New Home Price + Departing Price) * 0.75 - Current Mortgage)). Hard risk caps: 105% Acquiring LTV maximum and 75% Combined CLTV maximum.

**Tooltip popover never appeared on hover — real bug found and fixed (Confirmed 2026-09-22, found the day after the move above)**: the info-icon tooltip on this exact sentence (both `#roi-sentence` and `#g-roi-sentence`) silently failed to show on hover. Root cause was **invalid HTML, not a CSS bug**: the tooltip markup was `<span class="tip"><button>...</button><div class="tip-box">...</div></span>`, all nested inside a `<p>` tag (the sentence's own wrapping paragraph). A `<p>` element's content model only allows phrasing (inline-level) content — a block-level `<div>` is not permitted as a descendant, even nested inside an inline `<span>`. When a browser's HTML parser encounters a `<div>` while a `<p>` is still open, it auto-closes the `<p>` (and everything open inside it, including the `<span class="tip">`) **before** inserting the `<div>`, per the standard HTML parsing/error-recovery algorithm — so `.tip-box` silently ended up as a sibling of the (now-closed) `<p>`, a direct child of `#roi-sentence`, instead of nested inside `.tip` as written. Since `.tip-box` is `position: absolute` and relies on `.tip`'s `position: relative` as its containing block, this broke its positioning entirely — the popover rendered `display:block` correctly (so it wasn't invisible or undisplayed) but computed its position relative to a completely different ancestor, landing it far up near the top of the page, nowhere near the icon that triggers it, effectively making it look like "hovering does nothing."
  - **How this was actually diagnosed**: not from reading the CSS, but by forcing `.tip-box { display: block !important }` in a test copy to visually confirm the box rendered *somewhere* (ruling out "not rendering at all"), then injecting a small debug script to read `tipBox.offsetParent`/`parentElement` at runtime — which revealed `.tip-box`'s actual parent was `#roi-sentence`, not `.tip`, immediately pointing at the auto-closed-`<p>` HTML parsing behavior rather than any CSS positioning quirk (position:sticky and CSS Grid on ancestors were both suspected and ruled out first via the same debug technique, before landing on the real cause). **Any future "tooltip won't show" report should be diagnosed the same way** — force-display the `.tip-box` and check its actual DOM parent/offsetParent before assuming it's a CSS specificity or containing-block issue; if a `<div class="tip-box">` (or any other block element) is ever placed inside a `<p>`-wrapped sentence again, expect this same class of bug.
  - **Fix**: changed both `<div class="tip-box">` to `<span class="tip-box">` (block-level `display` still works fine on a `<span>` since CSS `display` isn't constrained by the element's HTML content-model category — only the parser's implicit-tag-closing behavior cares about the tag name). This keeps `.tip-box` correctly nested inside `.tip` inside the `<p>`, matching every other working tooltip in this file. Verified via the same offsetParent debug technique post-fix, and visually via a forced-display screenshot showing the popover now anchored directly above the info icon.
  - **`dti-buster.html` and `loan-officers.html` never had this bug** — when their own ROI-sentence tooltips were added (2026-09-21), they were built with `<span class="tip-box">` from the start (not copied from `index.html`'s then-buggy `<div>` version), so no fix was needed there. Confirmed by direct inspection of both files' markup.

The "Step 3 / Your Results" heading (`#results-heading` — this id was added specifically to make it a stable print-CSS target) spans both columns as its own row above them. This is done via `grid-column`/`grid-row` assigned directly to each existing element — **no new wrapper `<div>`s were added to the HTML**, keeping this purely a print-CSS concern that can't drift from the screen layout's DOM structure. `#results-panel > * + * { margin-top: 0 !important }` neutralizes the screen layout's `space-y-4`, which would otherwise add stray vertical offsets once the panel becomes a grid.
- **Only meaningful in the standard combo view.** The DTI-only ("dti-solo") card has no separate `#number-card` (no loan amount box exists for DTI-only), so it simply renders alone in column 1; column 2 (`#cost-benefit-card`, which doesn't exist in that mode either — DTI-only's cost/benefit numbers live inside `#gbc-dti-costs`, part of the same card as the title) ends up empty. No special-casing was added for this — it wasn't part of the request and degrades harmlessly.
- **Dropdowns are forced open**, since nobody can click a PDF: `#cost-breakdown`, `#sav-outer-body` (and their DTI-only equivalents `#gbc-cost-breakdown`, `#g-sav-outer-body`) get `max-height: none !important; overflow: visible !important` in print. **Only the two top-level accordions** — the nested per-item rows inside "Estimated Total Value" (`sav-cash-body`, `sav-staged-body`, `sav-move-body` and their `g-` DTI-only equivalents — `sav-hpa-body`/`g-sav-hpa-body` no longer exist, see the "Removed 2026-09-21" note above) are deliberately left collapsed, matching the reference design — "Net Benefit from Staging" / "Move Once, Not Twice" show as collapsed summary rows, not further expanded. Don't force those open too without a new design request. (Their chevrons used to be force-rotated 180° to *look* expanded — as of 2026-09-11 all chevrons are hidden outright instead; see the section below.)
- **Gotcha discovered while building this**: any element inside `#results-panel` that isn't given an explicit `grid-column`/`grid-row` gets auto-placed by the grid algorithm into whatever cell is left over after the explicitly-placed items claim theirs — which is often NOT where it visually belongs. This bit us with the "Step 3" heading itself (originally unplaced, it got shoved to the bottom of column 1 below the loan amount box) before `#results-heading` was given its own explicit `grid-row: 1`. If a new direct child is ever added to `#results-panel`, it needs an explicit placement rule here too, or it will land somewhere unexpected.
- **Second gotcha, fixed same day**: with `#cost-benefit-card` spanning `grid-row: 2 / span 3` (rows 2–4) opposite column 1's `#solution-card-wrapper` (row 2) and `#number-card` (row 3), the default `auto` row-track sizing algorithm distributed the *extra* height that `#cost-benefit-card` needs (it's much taller than the two column-1 cards combined) roughly evenly across all three spanned rows — which visibly pushed `#number-card` away from `#solution-card-wrapper` with a large gap between them, instead of leaving that surplus as trailing whitespace below column 1. Fixed by setting `#results-panel`'s `grid-template-rows: auto min-content min-content auto` — pinning rows 2 and 3 to `min-content` gives them no room to grow (their max growth limit equals their base size, so the spec's extra-space-distribution step skips them), forcing all of `#cost-benefit-card`'s surplus height onto the new trailing row 4 instead, which has nothing in column 1 so the extra space is invisible there. `#cost-benefit-card`'s span was extended from `span 2` to `span 3` to reach that new row 4. **If column 1 ever needs a 3rd stacked card, this pattern (one `min-content` row per column-1 card, plus one trailing `auto` row absorbing overflow, with column 2's spanning item reaching through all of them) needs to be extended the same way** — don't just add another `grid-row` number without also updating `grid-template-rows` and the span, or the gap bug will reappear between whichever rows are left as plain `auto`.

### PDF export redesigned as a static "loan estimate" document, not an app screenshot (Confirmed 2026-09-11)
The print stylesheet no longer just hides a few interactive bits of the live calculator — it actively removes anything that only makes sense on a clickable page, since this PDF is meant to read as a document a loan officer sends a buyer client, not a screenshot with dead UI in it:
- **`.tip` (the entire tooltip trigger — icon button *and* popup, not just the popup) is hidden in print**, everywhere, via one rule (was previously just `.tip-box`, which left the now-unclickable "ⓘ" icons visibly cluttering the page). This single rule covers every tooltip in the results panel, the savings items, and Step 1/2 — there's no need to enumerate them individually.
- **The "Details" label (text + chevron) on the four top-level accordion toggles is hidden via a shared `.details-toggle-label` class** added to those four spans (`#cost-benefit-card`'s and `#gbc-dti-costs`'s "Estimated Cost"/"Estimated Total Value" toggles). Any remaining bare chevron `<svg>` (the nested savings items — staging, HPA, moving, cash offer — which never had "Details" text next to them, just a value) is caught by a blanket `svg[id$="-chevron"]` attribute-suffix selector, so no per-item chevron needs to be listed by name.
- **Layout and colors are otherwise unchanged** — this was an explicit constraint. The 2-column Step 3 layout, dark "Estimated Max Loan Amount" card, amber warning boxes, etc. all look the same; only the interactive affordances (tooltip icons, chevrons, the editable transition-days text boxes) are gone, replaced with plain static text or short explanatory copy.

### Estimated Transition Days becomes its own top-level print section (Confirmed 2026-09-11)

### Fuller visual redesign referencing Flyhomes flyer templates (Confirmed 2026-09-11)
Later the same day, the "keep layout/colors unchanged" constraint from the header redesign above was explicitly lifted — the user asked for the PDF to look more deliberately designed, referencing the same three flyer PDFs (`Agent flier.pdf`, `Buyer flier.pdf`, `DTI Buster - Agent.pdf`) for inspiration, with freedom to add color/components/graphics (but no photos — there's no real asset to embed, and it wouldn't suit a document full of the buyer's own numbers anyway).
