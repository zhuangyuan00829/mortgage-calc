FAQ's

What exactly is the Guaranteed Backup Contract?

It's a legally binding backup purchase agreement that Flyhomes places on the homebuyer's existing home. Because it represents a committed sale, most underwriters allow the existing mortgage to be excluded from the DTI calculation.

How long does approval take?

Standard approval is within 24 hours. In urgent situations, we can often move in as little as 3 hours. No income documents, asset verification, or wholesale agreements required.

What happens if the old home doesn't sell?

If the home doesn't sell within 180 days of the new home closing, Flyhomes purchases it at the contract price. Flyhomes then handles the resale, and the homebuyer keeps any net profits from the eventual sale.

What's the cost?

The Guaranteed Backup Contract has a flat-tiered program fee starting at $2,500, based on the contract value. The fee can be covered by the homebuyer, the loan officer, or the real estate agent, or split between them.
