FAQs

What problems can you solve for me?

Whether you can't qualify while carrying your existing mortgage, need additional down payment, or want to compete like a cash buyer, there's a solution for that. Flyhomes DREAM™ Solutions are built for real situations, not just the easy ones.

How do I use Flyhomes' program?

Your agent or loan officer contacts Flyhomes, and we help select and guide the right solution for your situation from start to finish.

What if my current home doesn't sell?

If your home doesn't sell within 180 days, Flyhomes purchases it at the agreed-upon contract price. We manage the resale and any net profits* go back to you. *Net profits are calculated as extra proceeds minus Flyhomes' transaction costs, which cover items such as closing costs, holding costs, and realtor commissions.

What does it cost?

Fees vary by solution and are based on the loan amount, not the full home value. That difference alone can save you up to 80% compared to alternatives.
